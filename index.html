Sheeko tijaabo ah: Abaalmarin been ah

Akhriso lixda qaybood. Riix badhamada si aad u aragto casharrada. Boggan ma weydiisanayo PIN, password, telefoon ama xog bangi.

1. Farriin lama filaan ah
📱😮
“Hambalyo! Waxaad ku guulaysatay abaalmarin $1,000 ah. Riix si aad u qaadato!”

Su'aal: Maxaa shaki gelinaya farriintan?

Abaalmarin aanad filayn, degdegsiin, iyo ballanqaad lacag badan waa calaamado digniin ah.
2. Link aan la hubin
🔗⚠️
Link-ga tijaabada: example.invalid/prize
Ha furin link aan la hubin.
Ha riixin. Hubi cidda soo dirtay adigoo adeegsanaya app ama website rasmi ah oo aad adigu taqaan.
3. Bog xog kaa dalbanaya
🕵️‍♂️💻
Bog been-abuur ah wuxuu isku dayi karaa inuu kaa helo xog sir ah.

Ogow: Simulator-kan ma laha meel lagu geliyo PIN ama password.

Waligaa ha siin qof ama link aanad hubin PIN, password, OTP ama xogta akoonkaaga.
4. Iska ilaali khasaaraha
🚫💸
Qof ayaa ku leh: “Hadda xaqiiji si lacagta laguu siiyo.”
Jooji wada hadalka, ha dirin lacag, hana bixin koodh xaqiijin. La xiriir adeegga rasmiga ah.
5. Haddii aad link taabatay
😟📲
“Waxaan taabtay link aanan hubin. Maxaan sameeyaa?”
Xir bogga. Ha gelin wax xog ah. Haddii aad xog sir ah gelisay, isla markiiba beddel password-ka oo la xiriir bangiga ama adeegga rasmiga ah.
6. Sida loo badbaado
🛡️🌐
  • Hubi cidda farriinta soo dirtay.
  • Ha riixin link shaki leh.
  • Ha wadaagin PIN, OTP ama password.
  • Isticmaal app-ka rasmiga ah.
  • Daar xaqiijinta laba-tallaabo.
Hambalyo! Waxaad dhammaysay casharka wacyigelinta amniga internetka. Xogtaadu halkan laguma kaydinayo.
